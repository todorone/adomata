# Ad Account belongs to exactly one Client

Meta itself has no concept of Client and would happily let one Ad Account be granted to multiple parties, so nothing on Meta's side forces a one-to-one mapping. Adomata models it as one-to-one anyway — an Ad Account is scoped to exactly one Client, never shared — because Client is defined as "the boundary that campaigns, posts, and budgets are scoped to," and a shared Ad Account would make the Budget rollup (Client's monthly spend cap, summed across its Ad Accounts) ambiguous about whose cap the spend counts against. Many-to-many was considered and rejected for this reason. The schema should enforce this as a single foreign key from Ad Account to Client rather than a join table; if a genuine shared-house-account case shows up later, that's a deliberate migration, not a default to design around now.
