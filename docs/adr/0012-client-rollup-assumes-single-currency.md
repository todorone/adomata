# Client KPI rollups assume one currency per Client; mixed currency is unsupported, not converted

An Ad Account carries its own Meta-reported currency, and a Client can have more than one Ad Account. Adomata's Client-level KPI rollup sums Spend (and the values behind CPA/ROAS) across a Client's Ad Accounts with no currency conversion, on the assumption that all of a Client's Ad Accounts share one currency. A Client whose Ad Accounts actually use different currencies is an explicit unsupported state to detect and flag, not a case to silently sum through — the alternative, converting at rollup time, was considered and rejected for now because it opens a real design problem of its own (which rate, as of when, whose source) that nothing in the owner interview or CONTEXT.md has asked for. This is worth recording precisely because it's easy to miss: a future reader summing Spend across a Client's Ad Accounts might not realize the single-currency assumption exists until a mixed-currency Client produces a nonsense total.
