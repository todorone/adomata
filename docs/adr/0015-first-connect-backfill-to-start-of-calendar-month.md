# First-connect backfill covers the current month and Reconciliation Window

When an Ad Account is newly connected, its first sync backfills daily Insights history from the earlier of the first day of its current local calendar month or 28 days before its current local date, not just forward from the connection moment. The month boundary is required because [ADR 0002](0002-budget-scoped-to-client.md) tracks a Client's Budget Exhausted state against actual month-to-date spend; the earlier boundary is required because [ADR 0014](0014-daily-history-with-a-rolling-reconciliation-window.md) keeps the current day plus 28 prior days eligible for revision and the Last 7 days Time Range must remain complete across month boundaries. This is the minimum history that satisfies both rules without speculative deeper backfill.
