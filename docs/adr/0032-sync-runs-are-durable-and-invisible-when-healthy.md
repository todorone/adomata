# Sync runs are durable and invisible when healthy

Supersedes [ADR 0008](0008-two-tier-freshness-for-fleet-board.md) and [ADR 0016](0016-sync-driven-by-heartbeat-not-workers-native-cron.md). The old two-tier cadence and read-triggered fire-and-forget heartbeat made successful synchronization prominent while still allowing the board to display an old snapshot after the work finished. Adomata instead treats synchronization as durable per-Agency work: a repository-owned Cloudflare Cron Trigger creates or joins a run every five minutes, connection and Force Refresh wake the same runner immediately, persisted leases make interrupted work resumable, and Fleet Board reads only read Postgres. Account state, hierarchy, and today's Insights are independent per-account slices targeting five-minute freshness; the prior 28 complete account-local days reconcile nightly, while Creative enrichment never blocks operational data.

Healthy synchronization has no status copy or timestamps on the Fleet Board. The client quietly rereads its snapshot every minute and on focus, while Force Refresh remains an explicit Agency-scoped escape hatch and waits for a generation started at or after the request. An operational slice becomes Stale after ten minutes without success, and historical reconciliation becomes Stale after 36 hours. Degraded but usable data is marked with a yellow triangle; lost access, unavailable data, and other action-required failures use a red circle. Each icon opens an accessible Ukrainian detail popover with the affected slice, last success, cause, action, and diagnostic reference; only recovery clears it.

A newly selected Ad Account stays outside the Fleet Board until its Account data, complete hierarchy, today's Insights, and 90-day initial history are usable. Import progress persists and resumes independently per account; Creative metadata is non-blocking. Saving a valid replacement Meta token makes affected accounts retryable again. Routine operational work outranks Force Refresh, initial imports, and nightly reconciliation so backfills cannot make established accounts stale. Run details are retained for 30 days for diagnosis and a future reporting system; alert delivery itself is a separate decision.

Implementation is tracked in [issue #45](https://github.com/todorone/adomata/issues/45); synchronization reporting and alert delivery are deferred to [issue #46](https://github.com/todorone/adomata/issues/46).
